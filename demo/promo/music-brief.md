# Music brief for the tuiboard launch film (for Suno)

The edit follows the music, so what matters is a **steady tempo** and a **clear structure**.
Generate a handful of takes and keep the one with the cleanest beat; the video is cut to it.

## Style (paste in "Style of Music")

```
instrumental, 120 BPM, steady tempo, minimal electronic house, melodic techno, clean retro-futuristic synths, warm analog sub bass, tight crisp four-on-the-floor kick, offbeat hi-hats, plucky 16th-note arpeggio, airy pads, confident, focused, optimistic, modern software product launch, polished, no vocals, no tempo changes
```

## Structure (paste in "Lyrics", with Instrumental on)

```
[Instrumental]

[Intro - 4 bars - sparse airy pad, one soft pulsing synth tick, no drums, tension slowly rising]

[Build - 4 bars - plucky arpeggio enters, snare roll in the last bar, white-noise riser, filter opening, ends in one bar of near-silence]

[Drop - 8 bars - full groove, hard four-on-the-floor kick, offbeat hats, sub bass, bright arpeggio lead, big and clean]

[Break - 4 bars - drums out, pads and plucks only, half energy, a little space]

[Final Drop - 8 bars - peak energy, everything in, tight and punchy]

[Outro - 2 bars - drums cut, pad and one last pluck ring out, clean ending]
```

## Notes

- Length: 50 to 70 seconds is plenty. Longer is fine, the film uses one section of it.
- Ask for a **clean stop** at the end, not a long fade.
- Exact BPM is not needed (110 to 128 is fine); I detect the tempo and the downbeats from the file
  and snap every cut and every on-screen hit to them.
- Send me the file (mp3 or wav) wherever is easiest; a stem pack is a bonus, not a need.
- Suno's licence: a product film is commercial use, so check that the plan you generate on
  covers commercial rights before it goes public.
